# Market Hub — User Guide & Setup

Market Hub is a farm-to-table marketplace designed to connect local farmers directly with buyers.

## 🚀 How to Run the App

1. **Environment Setup**:
   - Ensure you have Python 3.11+ installed.
   - Activate the virtual environment: `venv\Scripts\activate` (Windows) or `source venv/bin/activate` (Linux/Mac).
   - Install dependencies: `pip install -r requirements.txt`.

2. **Database Migration**:
   - Run migrations to set up the database: `python manage.py migrate`.

3. **Running the Server**:
   - Start the development server: `python manage.py runserver`.
   - Access the app at: `http://127.0.0.1:8000/`.

---

## 👨‍🌾 Farmer Functionality (Seller)

### 1. Dashboard
- View quick stats: Total revenue, today's earnings, and low stock alerts.
- **Latest Products**: See your most recently added crops.
- **Incoming Orders**: See the latest 4 orders waiting for your action.
- **View All**: Click "View All Products" or "View All Orders" to see your full history with pagination.

### 2. Product Management
- **Add Product**: List new crops with price, unit, category, and stock levels.
- **Edit Product**: Update prices or restock existing items from your dedicated products page.
- **Stock Tracking**: The system warns you when stock is low (<= 5 units).

### 3. Order Fulfillment Flow
To keep buyers updated, follow this flow:
- **Accept Order**: Confirms you've seen the order.
- **Start Packing**: Moves the order to the preparation phase.
- **Ready for Delivery**: Notifies the buyer that items are ready for pickup or dispatch.
- **Confirm Delivery**: Finalizes the order and records revenue.
- **Reject**: Cancels the order and restores stock.

### 4. Earnings
- View detailed revenue breakdowns (Today, This Week, This Month, All Time).
- See your **Best Selling Products** by quantity and revenue.

---

## 🛒 Client Functionality (Buyer)

### 1. Marketplace
- Browse fresh produce from various farmers.
- Search by name, filter by category, farmer, or price range.
- Add items to your cart directly from the grid.

### 2. Dashboard
- View **Order History (Latest)**: Track the status of your 4 most recent purchases.
- **Fresh Produce (Latest)**: Quick access to the 4 newest items in the market.

### 3. Order Management
- **Cancel Order**: You can cancel "Pending" orders if you change your mind. Stock is automatically returned to the farmer.
- **Notifications**: Get real-time updates when a farmer changes your order status (e.g., "Ready for Delivery").

---

## 💡 Key Features
- **Itemized History**: Buyers can see exactly which items were in each past order.
- **Pagination**: The app handles large amounts of products and orders gracefully using pages.
- **Currency Formatting**: Clean, decimal-free UGX (or local currency) display.
- **Responsive Design**: Works beautifully on mobile and desktop.
